# Landing page redesign: papyrus by lamplight

The current landing page is correct in taste but flat: even lighting, no depth, no motion, and nothing on the page that shows what a reading actually looks like. Sage earns its substance through atmosphere, a live demo, and portrait-led density. We take those moves and translate them into the Bearing palette instead of copying its night-sky look.

## The look

Papyrus stays the page, but lit like a lamp on a desk:

- Layered paper: a radial warm-light pool behind the hero, a soft vignette at the edges, and two stacked paper tones (papyrus over papyrus-dark) so sections read as sheets rather than one wash.
- Ink washes and a fine plate texture over the whole page, plus faint engraved rules and register marks in the margins.
- Dark Lyceum bands used as punctuation between papyrus acts, with bronze hairlines where light and dark meet.
- Palette locked to the existing tokens: papyrus #E8D4A9, papyrus-dark #D9C08D, oxblood #682C27, dark lyceum #17140F, bronze #8A6A2F. Typography unchanged (Palatino display, Georgia body). No new colors, no new fonts.

## Act one: the hero with a living reading

Split hero. Left is the existing headline, tightened. Right is a framed specimen panel that runs a short, looping demonstration:

1. A row of concern chips (comparison, a decision, a pattern, resentment, dread, drift) with one active.
2. The matching question types itself into a ruled line, then a thin pause marker while the reading composes.
3. A real Bearing reading unfolds section by section: What stands out, The question, The principle, The practice, Sit with this. Sections reveal in sequence, then the panel resets to the next chip.
4. Clicking any chip jumps to that specimen; clicking the panel opens the real inquiry with that concern preselected.

The demo text is hand-written, held in a local file, and not generated by AI, so it costs nothing and never drifts off-brand. It respects reduced-motion by showing the finished reading with no typing.

## The rest of the page

- Concern strip under the hero: a horizontal band of the six concerns as pressable chips that open the inquiry, so the first action is a tap and not a paragraph.
- Lyceum rebuilt as portrait-led cards on the dark band: larger engraved portraits, method line, dates, a one-line "bring this here" prompt per philosopher, and a hover state that lifts the sheet and warms the plate. Locked philosophers show a bronze seal rather than a grey overlay.
- How it works reduced to three engraved numerals with short lines, set on a papyrus-dark sheet.
- Sample reading reframed as a full facsimile page: margin notes, section numerals, a folio number, and an oxblood drop initial.
- Membership block gets the specimen treatment: the two prices set as a ledger with a bronze rule, the benefit list as engraved entries.
- Footer gains a small compass seal and a colophon line.
- Motion throughout: reveal on scroll, a slow parallax drift on background plate marks, and hairline underlines that draw in on hover. Restrained, mechanical easing, nothing bouncy.

## Scope

Landing page only this round: hero, concern strip, why, Lyceum, how it works, sample reading, membership block, footer, nav, and the shared design tokens. The inquiry modal, reading view, membership checkout, auth, and dashboard keep working as they do now and get the same language in a later pass.

- New files: `src/data/specimens.ts` (demo concerns and readings), `src/components/SpecimenPanel.tsx` (animated hero panel), `src/components/ConcernStrip.tsx`, `src/components/Reveal.tsx`.
